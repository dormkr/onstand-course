# Week 32: Applications sprint I

**Block E · Outline**

## Why this week exists

By now you've been applying at a low rate since week 17. This is the high-volume sprint, timed for roles starting after your July graduation. The rule from your own ops repo applies here too: never pitch someone who can't say yes. A referral or a direct message to the hiring manager beats a cold application many times over.

## Tasks (15 h)

1. **Refresh the target list** (week 17's three tiers). Re-verify every company's location rule from its careers page, and date the check. Add companies you met through open source (weeks 16, 25) and mock interviews (weeks 11, 17, 29).
2. **30 tailored applications.** For each: one company-specific sentence, the most relevant case study linked, and the CV variant that fits (web, audio or general).
3. **At least 10 with a human connection:** a referral, a message to the hiring manager or an engineer on the team (via LinkedIn, the project's community, or the open-source PR you made), or an introduction from a mock interviewer. Your message: who you are in one line, one piece of evidence relevant to *their* product, one specific question. No attachments in the first message.
4. **Remote-hiring logistics:** know how you'd be employed (an employer of record such as [Deel](https://www.deel.com/) or [Remote](https://remote.com/), or a contractor agreement), and what that means for taxes in Kenya. Ask a Kenyan accountant once rather than guess.
5. **Track everything** in `jobs/PIPELINE.md`, with next actions and dates.

## Gate

- **w32.apps:** 30 tailored applications this sprint, ≥ 10 with a referral or direct contact.
