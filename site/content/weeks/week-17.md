# Week 17: Checkpoint 2, buffer, and applications begin

**15–21 February 2027 · Block C · Outline**

## Why this week exists

You graduate in July 2027, and companies hire graduates months ahead. Internship and new-grad cycles for summer 2027 are already open. Applications start now, at a low, steady rate, while you keep building. Waiting until you feel ready is how people end up applying in a rush in May.

## Tasks

1. **Mock interview #2** with a real engineer (a different person from week 11): coding, plus 15 minutes of "walk me through your hardest project". Get their written score.
2. **The application pipeline.** Create `jobs/PIPELINE.md` (or reuse the structure of your ops repo's `pipeline.md`: you already run a sales pipeline for freelancing, and this is the same discipline). One row per application: company, role, link, location rule (remote worldwide / EMEA / on-site), date sent, contact or referral, status, next action and date.
3. **Targeting.** Build a list of 30 companies in three tiers: (1) music software with remote-friendly hiring (start with [Muse Group](https://www.mu.se/careers), whose vacancies say "remote-first … 40+ countries"; then Yousician, BandLab, Moises, UJAM, Soundslice, Flat and Splice: verify each one's location policy yourself); (2) companies with audio or real-time web work; (3) good remote-first companies hiring juniors in any domain. **Verify every company's location policy from its own careers page**, and record the date you checked.
4. **CV v1 and a one-paragraph pitch**, using the [Tech Interview Handbook résumé guide](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/). Every line must link to evidence (a PR, a report, a recording). Put "EAT (UTC+3), full overlap with CET/EET" next to your location.
5. **Send the first 10 tailored applications** (internships and new-grad roles first). "Tailored" means one sentence that only that company could receive.
6. **The buffer:** use the remaining time for any late gates.

## The week-17 decision point

Is there at least one merged open-source PR, and have the first applications gone out? If there's no merged PR: week 25's PR becomes mandatory before any further applications. Record it in the build log.

## Gate

- **w17.mock:** mock #2 done, with a written score.
- **w17.apps:** 10 tailored applications in the pipeline.
- **w17.decision:** the decision point recorded.
