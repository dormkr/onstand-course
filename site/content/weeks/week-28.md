# Week 28: DSA intensive I

**Block E · Outline**

## Why this week exists

You've done about 3 problems a week since week 1, roughly 80 problems in total. Now you consolidate under interview conditions. Coding rounds aren't about having seen a problem; they're about recognising its pattern under time pressure, talking while you think, and testing your own code before the interviewer does.

## Method (15 h)

- **15 problems this week**, from the NeetCode roadmap sections you've done least well in (check `dsa/LOG.md`, and sort by time and misses). At least 10 should be mediums.
- **Conditions:** a timer (easy 20 min, medium 35 min), talking out loud while you solve (record audio for at least 5 of them), no AI, code that runs, and your own test cases written *before* you say "done".
- **After each one:** write the pattern in one line ("monotonic stack", "two pointers on sorted", "DP over prefix") and the mistake you made, in `dsa/LOG.md`.
- **Spaced repetition:** re-solve, from a blank file, three problems from earlier weeks that you'd marked as hard.
- Use the [Tech Interview Handbook coding guide](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-prep/) for the "how to talk during a coding interview" structure.

## Gate

- **w28.dsa:** 15 problems logged, mediums at ≤ 35 minutes, with solutions and pattern notes committed.
