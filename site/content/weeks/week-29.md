# Week 29: DSA intensive II and timed mocks

**10–16 May 2027 · Block E · Outline**

## Tasks

1. **12 more problems**, this time mostly graphs, heaps and DP, with the same conditions as week 28.
2. **Two timed mock interviews with real engineers** (Pramp, interviewing.io, or engineers from your network and communities). Ask each interviewer for a written score using their own rubric, and for the single thing that would most improve your next interview.
3. **One mock in your specialism:** ask an audio developer (the [JUCE forum](https://forum.juce.com/), The Audio Programmer Discord, [ADC](https://adc.io/) attendees, [ISMIR](https://ismir.net/) people) for a 30-minute technical conversation about your tuner benchmark or capstone. Treat it as an interview: they ask, you defend.
4. **Fix the top weakness** the mocks reveal, and repeat one problem type until it's fluent.

## Gate

- **w29.mocks:** two mocks with real engineers, both scored "hire" or better. If either is below, a third mock next week becomes part of this gate, and week 32's applications don't pause for it.
