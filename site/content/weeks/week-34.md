# Week 34: Applications sprint II and follow-ups

**Block E · Outline**

## Tasks (14 h)

1. **Follow up on every open application** older than 10 days: one short message, adding one new piece of evidence (a recent PR or case study), never just "checking in".
2. **20 more tailored applications**, prioritising companies where a human has replied before.
3. **Measure the funnel** in `jobs/PIPELINE.md`: applications → replies → first interviews → later rounds → offers, by tier and by channel (cold / referral / direct contact). Which channel works for you? Do more of that, and less of the rest.
4. **Interview loops in progress:** for each active process, write a one-page prep sheet: what the company makes, which of your projects matters most to them, three questions to ask, and the likely interview format (ask the recruiter; they'll usually tell you).
5. **Negotiation basics:** know your floor (based on your real costs in Nairobi and the local pay range), your target (the remote range for the role), and the exact words for asking for time to consider an offer.

## Gate

- **w34.callbacks:** the callback rate measured and reported in the build log; a follow-up sent on every open application.
