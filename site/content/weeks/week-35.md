# Week 35: Music-tech company deep dives

**Block E · Outline**

## Why this week exists

The candidates who get remembered at a small music company are the ones who clearly used the product, found a real problem in it, and did something about it before the interview. Generic enthusiasm doesn't count. This week you pick five target companies and make one artefact for each.

## Tasks (15 h)

For **five** companies (choose from your pipeline; the [direction call](#/direction) table is a starting point: Muse Group, Yousician, BandLab, Moises, UJAM, Soundslice, Flat and others you've found since):

1. **A dossier** (1–2 pages): what they make, who uses it, their tech stack (from job posts, engineering blogs, open-source repos), recent news, which team you'd fit, and who works there that you could talk to. Cite your sources and date them.
2. **A tailored artefact**, small but real, for example:
   - a merged PR or a well-written issue with a minimal reproduction in their open-source code (Muse Group: MuseScore or Audacity);
   - a teardown of their web player or practice feature: what it does with audio, measured (latency, pitch-detection behaviour on your flute vs OnStand's), written up respectfully;
   - a small demo that integrates with their public API or file format (MusicXML export, MIDI).
3. **A message** to someone on the relevant team that links the artefact.

## Gate

- **w35.dossiers:** five dossiers, each with a tailored artefact and a sent message.
